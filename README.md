# Counterexamples to two squared-codegree Turán conjectures

**Jialin He**

[Read the paper (PDF)](paper.pdf) · [LaTeX source](main.tex)

## Abstract

We study two five-vertex hypergraph Turán problems posed by Balogh, Clemen, and Lidický. For $K_5^{=}$, the complete three-uniform hypergraph on five vertices with two edges intersecting in one vertex deleted, a four-part construction disproves their squared-codegree density conjecture. It gives squared-codegree density at least $391686088/1029059101\approx 0.3806$ and improves the ordinary Turán lower bound to $(2235+76\sqrt{114})/5082\approx 0.5995$. For $J_4$, the suspension of $K_4$, we correct the count for the known iterated blow-up of the Fano complement and obtain squared-codegree density at least $16/57$, exceeding the proposed value $7/25$. We further prove that $16/57$ is optimal within this recursive family, allowing arbitrary part sizes and recursion depths. The proofs use explicit constructions, finite counting identities, and a nonnegative weighted-square identity.
